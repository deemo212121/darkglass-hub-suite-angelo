DARKGLASS HUB SUITE - PROGRESS UPDATE
Covering: July 17, 2026


NEW: FILLABLE TAX FORMS (W-4 / W-8BEN / W-9) - FILL, SIGN, AND TRACK IN-APP
-----------------------------------------------------
- New "W-8 / W-9 / W-4 Forms" HR tab. HR sends a link, the recipient
  fills in and signs the real official IRS PDF right in the browser
  (not a redrawn lookalike - the actual form, with entry fields placed
  exactly where they belong), and the completed form comes back to HR
  automatically.
- For the W-4 specifically, HR can go back afterward and fill in the
  "Employer's Only" section without needing the employee to resubmit
  anything.
- Added a COE (Certificate of Employment) Sent History log with a PDF
  preview, so HR can see what's already gone out.
- Fixed exported COE/Warning Form PDFs printing at roughly 4x their
  correct physical size - they now generate as proper US Letter pages.


NEW: JOTFORM SUBMISSIONS + ONE UNIFIED FILE SUBMISSIONS VIEW
-----------------------------------------------------
- Documents submitted through Jotform now automatically get pulled in
  as real PDFs, stored, and tracked - updating live, with the ability
  to soft-delete/restore.
- The Onboarding Documents tab now shows Jotform submissions and the
  new signed W-4/W-8BEN/W-9 forms together in one filterable list,
  instead of two separate panels.
- Added a bulk-upload option for documents that don't come through
  Jotform at all.


CERTIFICATE OF EMPLOYMENT REBUILT + CLICKABLE HR DASHBOARD TILES
-----------------------------------------------------
- COE rebuilt to match the official reference letter: new body text
  and Office Use box, honorific/pronoun fields, an Authorized
  Representative signer whose title/email now auto-fill on selection,
  and dates written out in full (e.g. "July 17, 2026").
- Every tile on the HR dashboard is now clickable and jumps straight
  to the relevant tab or filter - previously only the Attendance tile
  worked that way.


NSA TICKETS: COMMUNICATION LOG NOW SHOWS ON THE TICKET PAGE
-----------------------------------------------------
- NSA tickets now show their customer contact history (calls/texts
  plus service notes) on the ticket detail page, matching what
  ServicePower tickets already had.
- Caught partway through that NSA's own site actually combines two
  separate data feeds into that one log - fixed so ours matches
  entry-for-entry.
- Ended up removing the separate pop-up version of this once it became
  clear it was just showing the exact same information a second time -
  the inline Customer Notes section on the page was already covering
  it, and that section was given a scrollable, cleaner card layout so
  a long history doesn't push the whole page out.


NEW: NSA ESTIMATES PANEL ON THE TICKET PAGE
-----------------------------------------------------
- New panel showing an NSA ticket's approval status, total amount, and
  the Labor/Parts/Other breakdown, pulled live from NSA - verified
  against a real dispatch to match NSA's own numbers exactly.


NEW: NSA PARTS NOW SYNC AUTOMATICALLY, EVERY HOUR
-----------------------------------------------------
- Previously, pulling new parts in from NSA was a manual, one-off
  action - if NSA added a part after we'd already checked a ticket
  once, nothing here would ever know about it.
- The app now checks every open NSA ticket once an hour and
  automatically pulls in anything new, logging it the same way a
  manual pull already did (Change Log entry included). Deliberately
  scoped to parts only - NOT other ticket fields like status or
  schedule, since auto-overwriting something a staff member manually
  corrected would be a worse problem to introduce.
- Tested against real tickets, including a real "NSA added a part"
  scenario, before turning this on.


TICKET PHOTOS: FASTER UPLOADS, SMALLER FILE SIZES
-----------------------------------------------------
- Photos are now automatically resized and compressed before upload
  (targeting ~1MB each) instead of uploading the original, often much
  larger camera file. iPhone HEIC photos are now also handled
  correctly instead of failing to upload.
- Multiple photos now upload at the same time with live progress per
  photo, instead of one at a time - and if one photo fails, the rest
  of the batch no longer silently stops with it.


OTHER FIXES
-----------------------------------------------------
- Fixed a wrong-branch assignment bug: a customer in Villa Ridge, MO
  was being routed to a branch 137 miles away instead of the correct
  one 36 miles away, because the matching logic found a same-named
  town in a different state and didn't check that the state actually
  matched. Fixed for both NSA and ServicePower tickets, since they
  share this logic.
- Enlarged the mileage badge on the ticket page (previously easy to
  miss).
- Removed a duplicated Problem Description panel showing twice on NSA
  tickets.
- Fixed 3 Claims Readiness checklist items that were marking a
  freshly-pulled, not-yet-serviced ticket as "mostly complete" when it
  wasn't.
- Fixed the Automated Forms dropdown menu on the HR dashboard
  rendering behind other page content instead of on top of it.
- Continued hardening the deploy process against a Windows-only build
  failure (a leftover process holding a file lock during deploy) -
  deploys should now recover on their own instead of needing a manual
  retry.


TEAMMATE UPDATES MERGED IN
-----------------------------------------------------
